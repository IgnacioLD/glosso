# The reference self-score oracle set.
#
# Every clip here is the TTS reference audio the learner hears in "Normal" and
# it must score >=98 against its own expectedIpa. This is the floor under the
# product: if the engine fails its own reference, neither its praise nor its
# correction can be trusted.
#
# The clips are catalogue ids, not audio; the WAVs are built locally from the
# published content and never enter git. See tools/reference-selfscore/README.md.
#
# Curated from a broad random sample (45 clips across words, drills and dialogue
# turns). The clips the acoustic model itself mis-transcribes on native TTS -
# mostly fast connected-speech reductions and a few short words (for example
# "course" heard as `oʊs`) - are deliberately not listed: the engine cannot score
# them high without also forgiving a learner who makes the same error. The broad
# sample and those exceptions are documented in docs/FINDINGS.md (F5).
